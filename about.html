ABOUT
ASTRA

ASTRA is an influencer marketing agency focused on connecting brands with the right creators and helping influencers discover relevant brand collaboration opportunities.

Our goal is to make influencer marketing simple, professional and accessible for businesses of different sizes. We understand that every brand has different objectives, audiences and budgets, which is why we focus on finding suitable creators according to the specific requirements of each campaign.

At ASTRA, we work across the complete collaboration process. From identifying relevant influencers and understanding campaign requirements to communication, campaign planning and coordination, we aim to make the process smooth for both brands and creators.

We believe that successful influencer marketing is not only about follower count. The right audience, engagement, content style, niche and campaign requirements all play an important role in creating an effective collaboration.

For influencers, ASTRA focuses on bringing relevant promotional opportunities while maintaining flexibility and transparent communication. For brands, we provide access to suitable creators and flexible campaign options designed around their goals and budget.

Our vision is to build a reliable ecosystem where brands and creators can collaborate with clarity, professionalism and mutual value.

OUR PURPOSE

Connecting the right brands with the right creators while making the collaboration process simple, transparent and effective.
