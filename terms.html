02 — TERMS & SERVICES

TERMS &
SERVICES

Please read the following terms and services carefully before collaborating with ASTRA. These terms are designed to maintain a transparent, professional and secure working relationship between ASTRA, influencers and brands.

01 — FOR INFLUENCERS

Influencers collaborating with ASTRA will have complete freedom to work with other agencies, brands or management companies. ASTRA does not require exclusivity.


ASTRA will only receive a commission from promotions or campaigns that are successfully brought and coordinated by ASTRA. Any independent collaboration arranged by the influencer will not be subject to ASTRA's commission.


  • Influencers are free to work with other agencies.
  • No exclusivity is required with ASTRA.
  • Commission will only apply to campaigns arranged through ASTRA.
  • The influencer commission will generally be between 10% and 20%.
  • The applicable commission percentage will be selected by ASTRA according to the campaign value, pricing and requirements.

02 — INFLUENCER PAYMENT POLICY

For security and campaign completion purposes, ASTRA will provide an advance payment of approximately 20% to 30% of the influencer's agreed payment.


The remaining balance will be paid after the influencer has completed the required shoot and successfully uploaded the agreed promotional content.


This payment structure is intended to provide security for both the influencer and the campaign.

03 — FOR BRANDS

Any brand can collaborate with ASTRA for influencer marketing campaigns. Our campaigns are designed for businesses of different sizes and budgets.


Campaigns for brands start from just ₹1,499.


  • Brands can choose suitable influencers from the portfolio provided by ASTRA.
  • The influencer portfolio will be shared after the brand contacts ASTRA through WhatsApp.
  • Brands can select an influencer according to their campaign requirements and preferences.
  • A 40% advance payment is required from the brand before the campaign process begins.

04 — SCRIPT & SHOOTING

The promotional script will be prepared according to the campaign requirements and will be shared with the brand for review before the influencer records the content.


Shooting for campaigns will generally take place on Saturday or Sunday, depending on the influencer's availability and campaign schedule.


The influencer will proceed with the shoot only after the required script has been shown to the company/brand. This helps ensure that the promotional content follows the agreed campaign requirements.

05 — GENERAL TERMS

  • All campaign details, deliverables and payment terms will be discussed and agreed upon before the campaign.
  • Influencer selection and campaign coordination will be handled according to the requirements of the brand.
  • Any changes to campaign requirements should be communicated before the shoot.
  • By proceeding with a campaign, the concerned brand or influencer agrees to the applicable campaign terms communicated by ASTRA.

TRANSPARENT COLLABORATIONS

ASTRA believes in transparent communication, flexible collaborations and mutually beneficial partnerships. Our goal is to make influencer marketing simple, professional and secure for both brands and creators.
